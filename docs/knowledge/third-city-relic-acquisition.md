# Third-city relic acquisition

TASK-102. The third city is Elf Palace, with city index 2.

## Eligibility and sources

All retained production relic IDs remain physical items. This task does not adopt the proposed retirements or temporary-ball rewards in TASK-100.

- Merchant: all production relic IDs are in the candidate roster. City one offers Tier 1. City two offers Tiers 1 and 2. City three and later offer Tiers 1, 2, and 3.
- Wall reward: keep the existing lower-tier source pools. In city three, add every production Tier 3 relic to the board reward pool. This gives each Tier 3 item a free route before the final encounter.
- Boss reward: filter the existing boss pool by city. If it has no eligible items, use the existing lower-tier wall pools. The third-city boss pool becomes eligible while that city is current.
- Chest reward: keep the physical chest roster and apply the same Tier 3 city gate.
- Debug catalogs remain separate and can show all tiers. Old passive fields cannot activate effects.

The merchant route applies to every ID in the roster below. Every Tier 3 row also has the third-city wall-reward route. Earlier source pools keep their lower-tier entries; their presence does not make Tier 3 eligible early.

## Weights, cost, and timing

The existing rarity roll selects Tier 3 only on an epic roll when the city allows it. The third city's three wall steps use Tier 3 weights of 14%, 18%, and 23% per relic slot. A rare roll selects Tier 2. Common and uncommon rolls select Tier 1. Each offer excludes duplicate relic IDs.

Prices stay at 15, 25, and 40 gold for Tiers 1, 2, and 3. A refresh costs 5 gold. A player with 39 gold cannot buy Tier 3; 40 gold buys one item. Buying the same card twice must not deduct gold or add another item.

Gold income depends on play. The wall-breach gold helper has no production caller, so this plan does not count that value as guaranteed income. The first two wall rewards in Elf Palace provide free acquisition opportunities before its final wall. All Tier 3 relics can appear in those rewards. Merchant availability starts on entry to Elf Palace. Numeric price and encounter tuning remain with TASK-104.
