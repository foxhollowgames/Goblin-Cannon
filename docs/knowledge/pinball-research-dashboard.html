⚡ Playfield Component Taxonomy (In-Flight Mechanics)

Pop Bumpers

Active solenoids repelling the ball violently on contact. Injects high chaos.

Drop Target Banks

Plates that drop on hit. Completing target sets resets banks and lights rewards.

Ramps & Habitrails

Elevated wireform tracks for continuous speed and gravity-guided flipper returns.

Spinning Targets

Hinged plates spinning rapidly on ball passage. Triggers rapid switch ticks.

Scoops & VUKs

Subterranean holes that catch the ball, pause action, and launch balls to targets.

Electromagnets

Coils under playfield wood that capture, spin, or hurl balls in wild trajectories.

Captive Balls

Trapped steel balls in channels transferring impact kinetic energy to rear switches.

Mechanical Diverters

Code-controlled gate flappers redirecting balls inside ramps and orbits dynamically.
