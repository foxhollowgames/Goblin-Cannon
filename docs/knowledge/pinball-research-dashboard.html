🌀 The Digital Scoop Reservoir

How it Works: A specialized relic grid module that acts as an energy and ball accumulator. When a cannonball rolls into the Scoop Relic, it absorbs the ball and stores a copy of its current damage and elemental stats.

Massive Multiball Blast: When charged or triggered by player input, the Scoop Reservoir releases a massive burst of all stored cannonball copies at once across the playfield!
HIGH-IMPACT TARGET 2x2 Block / T-Shape

💥 Interactive Bash Toys

How it Works: Heavy central targets placed on the board (inspired by Castle Gates and Flying Saucers). They absorb direct impacts, display visual damage stages, and trigger massive area-of-effect explosions.

Physical Feedback: Rewards precise center shots with high kinetic impulse, repelling nearby balls while damaging boss units.

⚙️ Physical Mechanical Hardware Glossary (Background Reference)

Physical pinball machines use under-board solenoids and switches. This physical hardware terminology is included below for background reference:

Physical Scoop: A hole cut into wooden playfield boards where balls drop into under-board channels.
Physical Ball Lock: An under-table trough holding steel balls before releasing them.
VUK (Vertical Up Kicker): An under-board solenoid piston shooting balls up onto overhead rails.
